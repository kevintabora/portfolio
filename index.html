Communication capability program

Business problem

The organization relied on a third-party communication skills program that carried significant licensing costs and was not fully tailored to internal performance expectations, customer scenarios, or operational metrics. The challenge was not only to build a replacement program, but to create something leaders, trainers, and operational teams would adopt.

My role

  • Led needs and task analysis to define job-specific communication requirements
  • Designed the blended learning structure and scenario-based practice
  • Aligned content with performance metrics and business expectations
  • Developed internal materials to replace vendor-provided content
  • Supported adoption through leadership roadshows, campaign materials, trainer enablement, and rollout support

Solution approach

Analyze

Identified communication behaviors, task requirements, and performance expectations.

Design

Built an in-house blended program using realistic workplace scenarios.

Campaign

Supported adoption through leader roadshows, emails, videos, and stakeholder alignment.

Scale

Enabled trainers and operational teams to roll out the program across learner groups.

Adoption strategy

  1. Align with business metrics
  2. Secure leadership buy-in
  3. Launch awareness campaign
  4. Enable trainers
  5. Roll out to learner groups
  6. Embed into training and process expectations

Impact

Estimated $200K USD annual savings by replacing external licensing with an internal communication skills program.

  • Reduced dependency on vendor-provided training
  • Improved alignment between communication training and operational needs
  • Created reusable internal training assets
  • Supported consistent communication standards across teams
  • Strengthened trainer and leader ownership of the program

This case study is an anonymized summary. Proprietary names, visuals, materials, and internal data have been removed or generalized.
